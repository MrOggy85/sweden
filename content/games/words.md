Words the games say that are not pictures: questions, numbers, money. "swedish | english".

The code asks for these by their Swedish spelling, and `GAME_WORD_KEYS` in
scripts/gameContent.ts lists the ones it needs, so a missing row fails the build rather than
leaving a game silent. Numbers duplicate the topic page's: same word, same clip.

- ett | one
- två | two
- tre | three
- fyra | four
- fem | five
- sex | six
- sju | seven
- åtta | eight
- nio | nine
- tio | ten
- hur många | how many
- plus | plus
- är | is
- en | one
- krona | crown, one coin
- kronor | crowns, the money
- tack | thank you
- rum | room
