The kitchen itself: the pot and the cutlery in the cooking game. Same format as the other
game lists, "swedish | english | icon".

These are here rather than hardcoded in the component so they get clips from
`make generate-audio` and are checked by the build like every other spoken word. `gryta` is
the pot; the rest are tappable cutlery.

- gryta | pot | gryta
- tallrik | plate | tallrik
- gaffel | fork | gaffel
- sked | spoon | sked
- kniv | knife | kniv
